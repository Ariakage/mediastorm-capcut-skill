# MediaStorm Music Sound

Make sound an editorial layer with a reason to enter, change, and leave.

## Core workflow

1. Define the sound job before editing: establish place, bridge ideas, prepare a turn, support movement, release energy, or preserve silence.
2. Locate musical phrase boundaries and meaningful beats, then test cuts against the intended shot or story change. Treat auto beat markers as candidate coordinates only.
3. Preserve musical decay when a hard boundary would cut the tail. Where appropriate, extend the container or isolate a reverberant tail, then verify that the main phrase is not repeated and the tail is not clipped again.
4. Use J-cuts and L-cuts to create information or emotional continuity. Derive the lead or lag from the material, not from a universal number of seconds.
5. Choose ambience and designed effects from what the image implies. Use risers, low-frequency weight, and transitions only when they serve expectation, impact, or a narrative turn.
6. Check licensing and usage scope for music and effects. Treat AI-generated sound as a candidate source, not proof of quality or clearance.
7. Balance dialogue first, then music and effects around it. Distinguish measured loudness from perceived clarity, and avoid applying the same gain to every clip as a substitute for mix judgment.
8. Validate by listening through real transitions and the ending, including left/right integrity, room tone, tail decay, and whether effects mask speech.

## Boundaries

- This session did not complete original-audio listening. Present sound recommendations as course-informed planning until the actual mix is heard.
- `capcut loudness` can measure and optionally apply gain, but its command-level `mutates` flag does not capture the conditional `--apply` write; fetch the current contract first.
- `capcut render` is only a proxy preview and cannot prove final CapCut audio behavior.
- Do not invent universal J-cut lead times, fade durations, loudness targets, or platform requirements.

Read [references/sound-narrative.md](references/sound-narrative.md) for course-derived techniques, checks, and timecodes.

Use `mediastorm-dialogue-interview` for speech selection and `mediastorm-story-structure` for beat-level editorial intent.
