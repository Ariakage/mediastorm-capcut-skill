## Method and boundaries

- All 18 lessons were sampled at **2-second intervals**: 6,656 sampled frames and 1,658 timestamped 2×2 index sheets; every sheet was reviewed, and interface parameters were additionally checked with targeted full-resolution frame grabs and localized crops.
- This is **static sampling**: it can miss actions shorter than 2 seconds, intermediate frames of number flicker, and fast camera moves; it is neither frame-by-frame decoding nor continuous video input.
- **No audio-track listening was performed**: music, sound effects, tone, reverb, channels, and actual rhythm remain unaccepted.
- Course demonstration parameters are case values; models, prices, membership benefits, and platform options in the recorded interface do not represent the current version.
- This file uses `[sampled stills]` (picture) for what is directly visible, `[course narration]` (subtitle) for the instructor's narration, and `[pending verification]` (pending verification) for what still needs listening, continuous video, or CapCut interface confirmation.
